# 🏰 Guilds in EverQuest

Guilds are the backbone of EverQuest's social structure. More than just a chat channel, a guild provides community, organized raiding, shared resources, and friends who will help you through the toughest content in the game. This guide explains everything you need to know about guilds as a new player.

---

## 🤔 What Is a Guild?

A guild is a **persistent player organization** that groups characters together under a shared name and tag. Being in a guild gives you:

- 📢 A **private guild chat channel** (`/gu` or `/guild`)
- 👁️ Your **guild name displayed** under your character's name to everyone in the world
- 🏛️ Access to a **Guild Hall** (in later expansions) with amenities
- 🤝 A network of players to group with, ask questions, and share knowledge
- 📋 Guild ranks with different permissions
- 🗺️ The ability to participate in **guild-organized raids**

---

## 💬 Guild Chat

Once you're in a guild, use these commands to talk to your guildmates:

| Command | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| `/gu <message>` | Send a message to all online guild members |
| `/guild <message>` | Same as `/gu` |
| `/guildsay <message>` | Same as `/gu` |

Guild chat is visible to all guild members regardless of zone — it's a global channel within your server.

> 💡 Set up your [chat windows](chat-windows.md) to have a dedicated Guild window so guild chat doesn't get buried in your main window.

---

## 🏅 Guild Ranks

Most guilds have a rank structure. Common ranks (names vary by guild):

| Rank | Typical Permissions |
|------|-------------------|
| **Leader** | Full control — invite, kick, promote, set MOTD |
| **Officer** | Can invite members, manage events, sometimes kick |
| **Veteran / Senior** | Trusted long-term members, no special permissions |
| **Member** | Standard member — guild chat only |
| **Initiate / Recruit** | Probationary rank for new members |

Your rank determines what guild actions you can perform.

## 📝 How to Join a Guild

### Method 1: Get Invited
The most common way. A guild officer or leader sends you an invite:
```
/guildinvite <yourname>
```
You'll see a pop-up asking you to accept or decline.

### Method 2: Ask to Join
If you want to join a specific guild:
1. Look for their members in the world (their tag is visible under their name)
2. Send a polite `/tell`: *"Hi! I'm a level 20 Cleric looking for a casual guild. Is [Guild Name] recruiting?"*
3. Many guilds have a **recruitment officer** or post in zone chat

### Method 3: Respond to Recruitment Posts
Guilds frequently post in `/ooc` or `/shout`:
> *"[Fires of Heaven] is recruiting! Level 40+ all classes. PST Officer for invite."*

Respond with a `/tell` and introduce yourself.

---

## 🚪 How to Leave a Guild

```
/guildleader (check if you're the leader first — you should pass leadership before leaving)
/guildremove <yourname>
```
Or right-click your guild tag in the Guild window → Leave Guild.

> ⚠️ If you're the guild **leader**, you must **pass leadership** to another member before leaving, or the guild may become frozen with no one able to manage it.

## 🏛️ Guild Management Commands

| Command | What It Does |
|---------|-------------|
| `/guildinvite <name>` | Invite a player to the guild |
| `/guildremove <name>` | Remove (kick) a player from the guild |
| `/guildpromote <name>` | Promote a player one rank |
| `/guilddemote <name>` | Demote a player one rank |
| `/guildmotd <message>` | Set the Guild Message of the Day |
| `/guildstatus <name>` | Check a player's guild rank |
| `/who all guild` | List all online guild members |

## 🏠 Guild Halls (EQLive)

In **EQLive**, guilds can purchase a **Guild Hall** — a private instance with many amenities:

- **Banker** — guild bank access
- **Merchant** — buy/sell items
- **Guild Tribute Master** — contributes to guild benefits
- **Teleport pads** — fast travel to common zones
- **Crafting facilities** — forges, ovens, looms, etc.
- **Trophy displays** — show off raid achievements

Guild Halls require the guild to purchase a plot (costs significant plat) and are maintained through guild donations.

> 💡 Getting access to a guild hall as a new member is a significant convenience perk — guild halls save enormous time on banking, crafting, and travel.

## 🗡️ Raiding With Your Guild

Organized **raids** are the endgame of EverQuest — large groups of 12–72 players tackling the hardest content in the game:

- **Open World Raids:** Guild claims a camp (like Lord Nagafen or Lady Vox on P1999) and brings everyone
- **Instanced Raids (EQLive):** Private instances your raid force enters together
- **Raid Loot:** Distributed by officers/leaders — usually DKP (Dragon Kill Points) or loot council

As a new player, you likely won't raid immediately — but finding a guild that does guild-organized content gives you something to work toward.

## 🔍 Finding the Right Guild

Not all guilds are the same. Consider what you want:

| Guild Type | Who It's For |
|-----------|-------------|
| **Casual / Social** | You want friends, occasional grouping, no pressure |
| **Progression / Leveling** | Organized leveling runs, help gearing up |
| **Raiding** | Serious endgame — regular raid schedules, attendance requirements |
| **TLP / Season-specific** | Built around a specific server's progression schedule |
| **Tradeskill / Crafting** | Focuses on the crafting economy |

### How to Find a Guild
- Ask in `/ooc` in your starting zones: *"New player LFG casual guild, any recommendations?"*
- Check server forums on the EQ website or Daybreak forums
- Look at **EQ guild recruitment sites** — many servers have Discord servers with recruitment channels
- P1999 has active forums at project1999.com with guild recruitment threads

## 💡 Guild Etiquette

> 🤝 **Be active.** Log in regularly, participate in guild chat, and join group/raid events when you can. Ghost members who log in once a month often get quietly removed.

> 🙏 **Be gracious with loot.** Guild loot drama is real and can destroy communities. Pass on things you don't need, don't bid over your heads for items you can't use yet, and be transparent about what you need.

> 💬 **Communicate.** If you'll be away for an extended period, tell your officers. If you have a problem with another member, bring it to an officer privately first.

> 🆕 **Help new members.** Once you know more than they do, pay it forward. Nothing builds guild loyalty faster than a veteran taking 30 minutes to help a new recruit.

> 📊 **Understand DKP if your guild uses it.** DKP (Dragon Kill Points) is a currency earned by attending raids and spent on loot. If your guild uses DKP, learn the rules — new members often start at 0 and must attend events to earn bidding power.
