# ⭐ Alternate Advancement (AA) in EverQuest

Alternate Advancement — commonly called **AA** — is EQ's prestige system. Once you reach level 51, you can choose to earn AA points instead of (or alongside) regular XP, and spend them on powerful permanent abilities. AAs are one of the most impactful long-term investments in the game. This guide explains what they are, when to get them, and where to spend them first.

---

## 🤔 What Are AAs?

AAs are **permanent passive and active upgrades** to your character that exist outside the normal leveling system. Think of them as a second progression track that runs parallel to leveling.

- You earn **AA points** by gaining experience after level 51
- You spend points in the **AA window** on abilities specific to your class and universal improvements
- AAs persist forever — they don't reset when you level up, when you die, or under any normal circumstances
- A character with 1,000+ AAs is dramatically more powerful than one with 0, even at the same gear level

---

## 📅 When Can You Get AAs?

### Availability
AAs unlock at **level 51** (some servers vary slightly). Once you hit 51, you can open the AA window and start spending points.

### When *Should* You Start?
This is one of EQ's most debated questions. The general guidance:

| Server Type | Recommended AA Strategy |
|-------------|------------------------|
| **EQLive** | Start grinding AAs around level 51–55 before pushing to 60 |
| **TLP** | Varies — some players AA at 51–55, others rush to cap first |
| **P1999** | Many players AA seriously around level 55–60 |

> 💡 **The classic advice:** Spend some time at level 55–59 in solid XP camps banking AA points before pushing to 60. The game gets significantly harder without foundational AAs at endgame.

---

## 🔧 How AAs Work

### Opening the AA Window
Press **Alt+A** (or find it in your character menu) to open the **Alternate Advancement window**.

### Earning AA XP
You can toggle how much of your earned XP goes toward AAs vs. your regular level:
- In the AA window, set your **AA XP percentage** (e.g., 50% to level / 50% to AAs, or 100% to AAs)
- Setting it to 100% AA means you're not leveling at all — purely banking AA points
- Most players at 55+ set it to 100% AAs until they have the foundational set, then start leveling again

### Costs
AAs cost a variable number of points:
- Many basic AAs start at **1–3 points** per rank
- Higher ranks cost more — some abilities go to rank 5 or beyond
- "Crucial" AAs often cost 3–9 points total across all ranks

## 🏆 Universal AAs (Every Class Should Get These)

Regardless of class, these AAs are widely considered essential:

### Combat Stamina / Innate Stamina
- **Effect:** Increases your maximum HP
- **Why:** More HP = harder to die. Every class benefits. Get this first.

### Mental Clarity / Innate Intelligence/Wisdom
- **Effect:** Increases your maximum mana (varies by caster type)
- **Why:** More mana = more spells = more utility. Essential for all casters.

### Physical Enhancement / Innate Strength
- **Effect:** Boosts core combat stats
- **Why:** Straightforward power increase

### Natural Durability / Armor of Wisdom
- **Effect:** Increases your effective AC and survivability
- **Why:** Reduces how much damage you take from every hit

### Healing Gift / Healing Adept (Healers)
- **Effect:** Increases the effectiveness of your heals
- **Why:** If you're a healer, your primary job gets better. Top priority.

## 🎯 Class-Specific Priority AAs (Examples)

### Warrior
1. **Combat Fury** — increases double attack chance (more swings)
2. **Defensive Stance** — dramatically increases survivability
3. **Shield Block** — increases shield mitigation
4. **Taunt** improvements — better aggro holding

### Cleric
1. **Healing Adept / Healing Gift** — more powerful heals
2. **Divine Arbitration** — emergency group HP balancer
3. **Purify Soul** — removes debuffs from self

### Wizard
1. **Mental Clarity** — more mana
2. **Fury of Magic** — increases critical spell hit chance
3. **Spell Casting Fury** — more critical nukes

### Rogue
1. **Combat Fury** — more attacks
2. **Ambidexterity** — improves dual-wield
3. **Ferocity** — chance for additional attacks

### Enchanter
1. **Mana Preservation** — spells cost less mana
2. **Mental Clarity** — bigger mana pool
3. **Hastened Mezmerization** — faster recast on mez

### Necromancer
1. **Mental Clarity** — more mana
2. **Pet Affinity** — buffs affect your pet
3. **Death's Effigy** — powerful combat AA

### Monk
1. **Combat Fury** — more attacks
2. **Purify Body** — removes poisons and diseases from self
3. **Innate Camouflage** — improved hide/sneak

> 💡 For detailed class-specific AA priorities, check the **EQ wiki** or **class-specific forums** — the AA landscape changes with each expansion.

## 📈 How Many AAs Should You Have?

Rough benchmarks at level 60 (classic era):

| AA Count | Status |
|----------|--------|
| 0–50 | New to AAs — missing significant power |
| 50–150 | Getting established — core abilities filling in |
| 150–300 | Solid AA base — competitive for most group content |
| 300–500 | Well-AA'd — ready for serious raiding |
| 500–1000+ | Veteran — heavily invested |

On **EQLive** with modern expansions, top players have **10,000+ AAs**. On classic TLP/P1999 the cap is much lower since fewer AAs are available.

---

## 🏕️ Best AA Grinding Spots

You earn AAs from any XP-granting kills, so the best AA camps are high-density, fast-respawn zones where you can kill safely and efficiently. At level 55–60:

| Zone | Notes |
|------|-------|
| **Velketor's Labyrinth** | Solid trash mob density |
| **Karnor's Castle** | Classic AA grind spot |
| **Sebilis** | Good group camps |
| **Planes of Power zones** (EQLive) | After Planes of Power expansion unlocks |
| **Old Sebilis** | Strong XP at the right level |

The key is **consistent, fast kills** — not necessarily the hardest mobs. A mob that dies in 15 seconds yields more AAs per hour than a tough mob that takes 3 minutes.

## ⚡ AA Quick Reference

```
Alt+A              Open the AA window
Set XP %           In AA window — controls how much XP goes to AAs
Level 51           When AAs unlock
First buy          Combat Stamina (HP) and class core abilities
Goal before 60     100–200 AAs depending on class
/aaxp              Check your current AA XP% setting
```

> 💡 **Don't wait until 60 to start.** Players who hit level 60 with 0 AAs find the endgame significantly harder. Grind 50–100 AAs around levels 55–58 before pushing to cap.
