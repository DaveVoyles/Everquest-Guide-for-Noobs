## ⚔️ Classes Overview

EverQuest has 16 classes divided into five archetypes. Understanding your role before you create a character will save you a lot of confusion in the early game.

---

### 🛡️ Tanks

Tanks hold the attention of enemies (*aggro*), absorb damage, and keep their group alive by standing between the monsters and the squishier members of the party.

---

#### ⚔️ Warrior

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Pure melee tank — the best at holding aggro and surviving big hits |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Easy (straightforward to play; hard to master) |
| **Armor** | All armor types including plate |
| **Weapons** | All weapons |

**Key Strengths:**
- ❤️ Highest HP and AC of any class
- Best defensive skills (defense, riposte, dodge, parry)
- Dual-wield and double-attack at high levels
- Taunt ability to pull aggro reliably

**Key Weaknesses:**
- No spells whatsoever — entirely dependent on healers
- Very weak solo capability, especially at lower levels
- Almost useless without a group
- No utility; purely a damage sponge

**Best for:** Players who enjoy being the group anchor, coordinating pulls, and being the reliable foundation that holds everything together. Rewards those who learn encounter mechanics deeply.

---

#### 💀 Shadowknight

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Dark magic–wielding hybrid tank with self-sustain and solid DPS |
| **Difficulty** | 🟡 Medium |
| **Armor** | All armor types including plate |
| **Weapons** | Most melee weapons |

**Key Strengths:**
- Spells including lifetaps (steal HP from enemies to heal yourself)
- 💀 Feign Death — fake dying to drop aggro and escape danger (a lifesaver)
- Harm Touch — massive once-per-72-hour nuke
- More solo-capable than Warrior due to lifetaps
- Fear spells can kite enemies in the open

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Lower AC than a pure Warrior
- Restricted to evil alignments
- Mana pool is secondary — you're still primarily a melee fighter
- Exp penalty in classic EQ due to being a powerful hybrid class

**Best for:** Players who want a tanky character that can also function solo and has a dark, necromantic flavor. Great for players who hate being helpless without a group.

---

#### 🛡️ Paladin

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Holy warrior hybrid tank with healing and utility |
| **Difficulty** | 🟡 Medium |
| **Armor** | All armor types including plate |
| **Weapons** | Most melee weapons, emphasis on 1H + shield |

**Key Strengths:**
- Can cast heals — invaluable in an emergency with no cleric
- 🛡️ Lay on Hands — massive once-per-72-hour self-heal
- Undead-killing abilities (Stun, Turn Undead, Holy spells)
- Group buffs including damage shields and resistances
- Excellent in undead-heavy zones

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Restricted to good alignments
- Not as high DPS or tanking ability as pure Warrior
- Spells are helpful but limited compared to a pure priest
- Exp penalty like Shadowknight

**Best for:** Players who want a supportive, team-oriented tank. Excellent in groups especially where undead are present. Great for players who enjoy knowing they can patch-heal if needed.

---

### ⚔️ Melee DPS

Pure damage dealers who focus on dealing as much damage as possible in melee range. They depend on tanks to hold aggro and healers to keep them alive.

---

#### 🥷 Monk

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Fast-attacking unarmed/blunt melee DPS with utility skills |
| **Difficulty** | 🟡 Medium |
| **Armor** | Leather and lower (weight matters!) |
| **Weapons** | Hand-to-hand, blunt weapons |

**Key Strengths:**
- 💀 Feign Death — one of the most valuable utility skills in the game
- Excellent puller thanks to Feign Death; standard group puller
- High sustained DPS with fast attack speeds
- Mend skill to self-heal on a timer
- Flying Kick and other special attacks

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Carrying too much weight severely penalizes combat effectiveness — gear management is critical
- No armor heavier than leather
- Low HP compared to tanks
- Requires learning weight limits and pull techniques

**Best for:** Players who enjoy an active, skill-intensive playstyle and want to be the group's puller. Monks are highly valued and always in demand.

---

#### 🥷 Rogue

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Backstab specialist with the highest burst melee DPS |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Easy (combat) / 🟡 Medium (positioning) |
| **Armor** | Leather |
| **Weapons** | Piercing and slashing weapons (must be behind target to Backstab) |

**Key Strengths:**
- Backstab — massive damage when attacking from behind
- Highest single-target melee DPS in game when positioned correctly
- Sneak and Hide for sneaking past enemies
- Evade skill to reduce aggro
- Poison crafting (Tradeskill) for extra damage

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Must attack from behind for maximum damage; positioning matters enormously
- No spells, no self-heal
- Fragile — dies quickly if aggro is pulled
- Nearly helpless if soloing (no escape, no heal, no range)

**Best for:** Players who enjoy maximizing pure damage output and playing the assassin archetype. Excels in groups where the tank reliably holds aggro.

---

#### 🧨 Berserker

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Two-handed axe specialist and the game's pure melee DPS powerhouse |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Easy |
| **Armor** | Chain armor |
| **Weapons** | Two-handed axes and slashing weapons; Frenzy attack chain |

**Key Strengths:**
- Frenzy — a unique attack that, unlike regular hits, cannot miss
- High burst and sustained damage with 2H weapons
- Adrenaline Rush for self-buffing in combat
- Simple, straightforward gameplay loop

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Limited to Barbarian and Dwarf races (originally), expanded later
- No utility — no sneak, no feign, no spells
- Entirely reliant on the group for survival
- Struggles to solo at most levels

**Best for:** Players who want to deal massive damage without worrying about complex rotations or positioning. The most "hit things until they die" class in EverQuest.

---

### ⚔️🔮 Hybrid Melee/Magic

These classes blend melee combat with spellcasting, granting flexibility and solo capability at the cost of being slightly behind pure specialists.

---

#### 🏹 Ranger

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Ranged and melee hybrid with tracking, buffs, and nature magic |
| **Difficulty** | 🟡 Medium |
| **Armor** | Chain armor |
| **Weapons** | Archery (bows), dual-wield melee |

**Key Strengths:**
- 🔑 Tracking skill — shows every nearby creature and player on a compass list; invaluable for finding named mobs
- Archery DPS (especially powerful in later expansions with focus effects)
- Nature spells: heals, damage shields, snare, ports
- Good solo capability with snare + kiting
- Dual-wield melee as a backup

**Key Weaknesses:**
- "Jack of all trades, master of none" — not as good at archery as a pure nuker, not as good in melee as a Rogue
- Classic EQ Rangers were notoriously underpowered in melee until later expansions fixed archery
- Exp penalty as a hybrid
- Equipment costs (arrows, bows) can be expensive

**Best for:** Players who enjoy exploring, hunting named monsters, and having options in combat. The Tracking skill alone makes Rangers welcome in almost every group.

---

#### 🐾 Beastlord

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Pet-using melee hybrid with a permanent animal companion and shaman-like buffs |
| **Difficulty** | 🟡 Medium |
| **Armor** | Leather |
| **Weapons** | Hand-to-hand, blunt, and some others |

**Key Strengths:**
- 🐾 Warder pet — a permanent combat pet that fights alongside you at all times
- Shaman-lite spells: slow, minor heals, haste, buffs
- Good solo capability (pet + heals + slow)
- Unique class with a very engaging playstyle
- Available to Iksar, Vah Shir, Ogre, Troll, and Barbarian

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Pet management adds complexity
- Not as powerful a caster as a Shaman; not as strong a melee fighter as a Monk
- Leather armor limits survivability
- Later addition to the game (Luclin expansion) — may not be available on all progression servers

**Best for:** Players who want a companion animal, enjoy multitasking (managing pet + casting + meleeing), and want strong solo ability with good group utility.

---

### 💊 Priests

The healers of EverQuest. Without priests, groups die. All three have very different flavors beyond healing.

---

#### 💊 Cleric

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | The primary healer — best heals in the game, period |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Easy (healing role is straightforward) |
| **Armor** | Plate armor |
| **Weapons** | Blunt weapons only |

**Key Strengths:**
- 💊 Best heals in the game — no other class comes close
- Complete Heal (CH) — the cornerstone of high-level group healing
- Resurrection spells to bring the dead back with experience restored
- Group buffs (Symbol, Armor of Protection)
- Undead nukes and Turn Undead

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Almost no solo capability — very mana-dependent, low DPS
- Blunt weapons only
- Heavily dependent on mana; downtime between fights for med (meditation)
- Being the healer means the group lives or dies on your focus

**Best for:** Players who enjoy the support role and the satisfaction of keeping everyone alive. Clerics are *always* in demand — you will never wait long for a group invitation.

---

#### 🌿 Druid

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Versatile nature caster — heals, damage, ports, and outdoors utility |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Easy |
| **Armor** | Leather armor |
| **Weapons** | Blunt and some piercing |

**Key Strengths:**
- ✨ Teleportation spells — can port entire groups to dozens of destinations; extremely valuable
- Solid heals (not as strong as Cleric but sufficient)
- Damage-over-time (DoT) and nukes for solo play
- Snare + kiting ability for solo grinding
- Outdoor tracking (lesser than Ranger but useful)
- Succor (emergency group evacuation)

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Heals are inferior to Cleric — at high levels, groups prefer Clerics for serious content
- No plate armor — squishier than a Cleric
- Spells mostly effective outdoors; weaker in dungeons

**Best for:** Players who want a flexible, independent character that can solo effectively, contribute to groups, and provide travel utility. One of the best classes for new players who want to explore the world.

---

#### 🥁 Shaman

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Buff/debuff specialist priest with the game's most powerful group buffs |
| **Difficulty** | 🟡 Medium |
| **Armor** | Chain armor |
| **Weapons** | Blunt and some others |

**Key Strengths:**
- 🔑 Slow spell — reduces enemy attack speed by up to 75%; arguably the most powerful debuff in the game
- Haste (Spirit of Wolf, etc.) — speeds up group members' attack speeds significantly
- Strong heals and HoTs (heals over time)
- DoT spells for solo efficiency
- Cannibal spells to convert HP to mana
- Pet (Spirit) for solo use

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Slow is so critical that groups often *require* one — the pressure to always have it ready can be stressful
- More complex than Cleric due to juggling buffs, debuffs, and heals
- Exp penalty as a hybrid

**Best for:** Players who want to be a team force multiplier. A Shaman with Slow active effectively makes every fight easier. Great for players who enjoy the support/buff role beyond just healing.

---

### 🔮 Casters

Pure spellcasters who deal damage, control the battlefield, or support through arcane means. They wear cloth or leather, are fragile, but their spells are devastating.

---

#### 🧙 Wizard

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Highest burst magical damage dealer — the glass cannon |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Easy |
| **Armor** | Cloth |
| **Weapons** | Blunt only |

**Key Strengths:**
- Highest burst damage of any class with nuke spells
- Teleportation spells (different destinations than Druid, covering between them most of the world)
- Excellent kiting with snare + run speed buffs
- Portal spells for fast travel

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Fragile — dies in a few hits
- Mana-intensive; significant downtime medding between fights
- Solo capability limited by mana — nukes are expensive
- No debuffs, no heals, no utility beyond ports and damage

**Best for:** Players who want to deal massive damage and enjoy the satisfaction of deleting enemies with big spells. Teleports make Wizards popular travel companions.

---

#### 🔮 Magician

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Pet class specializing in fire/summoning magic with self-sufficient soloing |
| **Difficulty** | 🟢 Easy |
| **Armor** | Cloth |
| **Weapons** | Blunt only |

**Key Strengths:**
- Earth, Fire, Water, and Air pets — four elemental pets to choose from, each with different strengths
- Can summon food, water, bandages, and even weapons/armor for the pet
- Completely self-sufficient: pet tanks, Magician nukes from range
- Among the best solo classes in the game
- Pet can be summoned with armor you craft via Imbue spells

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Pet management adds a layer of complexity
- Cloth armor — dies quickly if enemies reach you
- Less group utility compared to Enchanter or Necromancer
- Pet dies and must be re-summoned

**Best for:** New players who want strong solo capability without worrying about finding a group. The Magician + Earth pet combo is one of the most forgiving leveling experiences in EverQuest.

---

#### 💀 Necromancer

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Death magic specialist with the best solo capability in the game |
| **Difficulty** | 🟡 Medium |
| **Armor** | Cloth |
| **Weapons** | Blunt only |

**Key Strengths:**
- 💀 Best solo class in the game — DoTs, undead pet, lifetap, Feign Death
- Feign Death (like Monk) — can escape almost any situation
- Undead pet for tanking
- Lich spells that convert HP to mana (dangerous but efficient)
- Can charm undead temporarily for additional pets
- Fear kiting — one of EQ's famous solo techniques

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Evil-only — starting zones are hostile to you in many good cities
- Feared and disliked by NPC guards/citizens in many areas
- Complex — juggling DoTs, pet, lifetaps, and Feign Death requires focus
- Not as valued in groups compared to other casters (Enchanters preferred)

**Best for:** Players who want to solo effectively throughout the game and enjoy the dark arts. Necromancers are legendary for their ability to take on content alone that other classes can't.

---

#### 🧙 Enchanter

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Crowd control and buff specialist — the most powerful support class |
| **Difficulty** | 🔴 Hard |
| **Armor** | Cloth |
| **Weapons** | Blunt only |

**Key Strengths:**
- 🔑 Mez (Mesmerize) — stuns multiple enemies, preventing them from fighting; the gold standard of crowd control
- Haste spells — the fastest haste in the game, dramatically boosting group DPS
- Clarity/Brilliance — mana regeneration buffs that every caster wants
- Charm — convert monsters to temporary allies; the most dangerous and rewarding mechanic in EQ
- Tashani — reduces enemy magic resistance, enabling spell chains
- The most sought-after class for high-end group content

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Extremely punishing to play poorly — a failed mez in a multi-mob fight can wipe the group instantly
- No direct healing; entirely reliant on others to survive
- Charmed pets can break and attack you
- Very high learning curve — arguably the hardest class to play well

**Best for:** Experienced players who want to master the most complex and rewarding class in EQ. A skilled Enchanter is worth two of almost any other class.

---

#### 🎵 Bard

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Role** | Song-singing hybrid support class — the ultimate jack of all trades |
| **Difficulty** | 🔴 Hard |
| **Armor** | Chain armor |
| **Weapons** | Most weapon types |

**Key Strengths:**
- Songs that affect the entire group: haste, mana regen, HP regen, movement speed, see-invisible, resist buffs
- 🎵 Can *twist* (cycle) multiple songs simultaneously — the defining Bard skill
- Mez songs for crowd control
- Charm songs
- Snare and speed songs enable kiting
- Excellent puller with Fading Memories (instant aggro drop)
- Unique: songs do not consume mana (they use an instrument skill system instead)

**Key Weaknesses:**
- Twisting songs requires fast, rhythmic keypresses every 3 seconds — incredibly demanding in real-time
- Not great at any one thing; excels only when doing many things at once
- Complex to play optimally; easy to play poorly
- Combat effectiveness relies heavily on the player's execution

**Best for:** Players who want the most active, complex, and rewarding gameplay in EverQuest. Bards who master song twisting are invaluable in any group composition.

## 🌍 Races Overview

EverQuest's races each have different starting stats, racial abilities, starting cities, and class restrictions. Your race affects early gameplay significantly but becomes less impactful as you gain levels and gear.

> **Stat Note:** STR = Strength, STA = Stamina (HP), AGI = Agility (AC, avoidance), DEX = Dexterity (attack, skills), WIS = Wisdom (Cleric/Druid/Shaman mana), INT = Intelligence (Wizard/Magician/Necromancer/Enchanter mana), CHA = Charisma (Bard, merchant prices, Enchanter power)

---

### 🧑 Human

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Qeynos (good) or Freeport (neutral/evil) |
| **Alignment** | Good, Neutral |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Vision** | Normal (no infravision) |

**Description:** Balanced across all stats with no particular strengths or weaknesses. Humans are the most class-flexible race in the game, able to play every class available to them. Their perfectly balanced stats make them a solid choice for any role, though they won't excel as dramatically in any single stat as a specialized race.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- No special abilities
- Most class options available
- Good faction standing in most cities

---

### 💪 Barbarian

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Halas |
| **Alignment** | Good, Neutral |
| **Size** | Large |
| **Vision** | Normal |

**Description:** Large, powerful northerners from the frozen city of Halas. Barbarians have the highest base Strength and Stamina of the "good" races, making them natural Warriors and Shamans. Their size is a double-edged sword — large characters can't fit through certain small openings in dungeons.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- High STR and STA base stats
- Can play Berserker (one of few races that can)
- Natural cold resistance
- Rage ability (brief enrage)

---

### 💀 Dark Elf

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Starting City** | Neriak |
| **Alignment** | Evil |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Vision** | Ultravision (see in the dark better than Infravision) |

**Description:** Dark-skinned, innately magical elves from the underground city of Neriak. Dark Elves have high Intelligence and are the premier choice for evil-aligned caster classes. They are hated on sight by most good-aligned NPCs but have excellent faction in evil cities and have the unique benefit of Ultravision.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- Very high INT — best caster race for evil characters
- Ultravision — superior darkness vision
- Hide ability (limited)
- Can play Shadowknight, Necromancer, Wizard, Magician, Enchanter, Cleric, Warrior, Rogue

---

### 🏔️ Dwarf

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Kaladim |
| **Alignment** | Good |
| **Size** | Small |
| **Vision** | Infravision |

**Description:** Stout, sturdy craftspeople from the underground city of Kaladim. Dwarves have high Stamina, Strength, and Wisdom, making them excellent Clerics and Warriors. Their small size allows them to fit through tight spaces in dungeons that Large races cannot.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- High STA and WIS — natural Clerics
- Can play Berserker (one of few races)
- Infravision
- High alcohol tolerance
- Good faction in dwarven-affiliated areas

---

### 🧠 Erudite

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Erudin (good) or Paineel (evil) |
| **Alignment** | Good, Evil |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Vision** | Normal (no infravision — a notable weakness) |

**Description:** Tall, intellectual descendants of ancient Human scholars. Erudites have the highest base Intelligence in the game — the best choice for any INT-based caster class. They pay for this with very low Strength and Agility. They lack infravision, which is a meaningful handicap in dark dungeons.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- Highest INT in the game — best caster race overall
- Innate magic resistance
- Can play Wizard, Magician, Necromancer, Enchanter, Cleric, Paladin, Shadowknight
- No infravision — requires a torch or spells in dark areas

---

### 🔧 Gnome

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Ak'Anon |
| **Alignment** | Good, Neutral |
| **Size** | Small |
| **Vision** | Infravision |

**Description:** Tiny, inventive tinkerers from the mechanical city of Ak'Anon. Gnomes have high Intelligence and Dexterity, making them strong casters and Rogues. Their small size is advantageous in dungeons. They're the only race with Tinkering as a tradeskill — capable of crafting gadgets including temporary items.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- High INT and DEX
- Tinkering tradeskill (unique to Gnomes)
- Infravision
- Small size — fits through tight dungeon passages
- Can play Wizard, Magician, Necromancer, Enchanter, Cleric, Warrior, Rogue, Paladin, Shadowknight

---

### 🧝 Half Elf

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Qeynos, Freeport, or Kelethin |
| **Alignment** | Good, Neutral |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Vision** | Infravision (low-light; weaker than true elf) |

**Description:** Children of Human and Elven parents, Half Elves are generalists who gain some Elven grace and Infravision. Their stats are a middle ground between Humans and Elves — not exceptional at anything, but capable in many roles. Popular choices for Bards and Rangers due to their balanced stats.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- Partial infravision
- Balanced stats — good generalist race
- Can play Ranger, Paladin, Warrior, Cleric, Rogue, Druid, Bard
- Multiple starting city options

---

### 🏃 Halfling

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Rivervale |
| **Alignment** | Good |
| **Size** | Small |
| **Vision** | Infravision |

**Description:** Small, nimble, and inherently lucky folk from the pastoral city of Rivervale. Halflings have the best base Agility and Dexterity of any small race, making them outstanding Rogues. They also have a unique racial Hide and Sneak ability that starts stronger than most other races. Their small stature and good nature make them popular.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- Highest AGI and DEX — best Rogue race
- Natural Hide and Sneak skills (start at a higher level)
- Infravision
- Small size
- Can play Warrior, Cleric, Druid, Rogue, Ranger, Paladin

---

### 🧝 High Elf

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Felwithe |
| **Alignment** | Good |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Vision** | Infravision |

**Description:** Noble, graceful elves from the gleaming city of Felwithe. High Elves have high Intelligence and Wisdom, making them excellent Clerics, Enchanters, Magicians, and Wizards. They are the quintessential good-aligned magical race. Their innate grace and magic affinity are reflected in strong mana-related stats.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- High INT and WIS — versatile caster race
- Infravision
- Good faction throughout Faydwer
- Can play Cleric, Paladin, Wizard, Magician, Enchanter, Warrior

---

### 🐉 Iksar

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Cabilis (Kunark expansion) |
| **Alignment** | Evil |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Vision** | Infravision |

**Description:** Reptilian humanoids from the ancient empire of Kunark. Iksar are natural fighters and shamans with exceptional natural AC (scales), regeneration abilities, and strong Wisdom. They are universally hated by nearly every other race and cannot enter most cities without faction grinding. Added in the Kunark expansion.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- Natural AC bonus (scales)
- ❤️ Superior regeneration (out of combat HP regen)
- High WIS — excellent Shaman race
- Swim skill bonus
- Can play Monk, Shadow Knight, Shaman, Warrior, Necromancer, Beastlord
- *Extremely* poor faction everywhere outside Kunark — challenging for new players

---

### 💪 Ogre

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Oggok |
| **Alignment** | Evil |
| **Size** | Large (very large) |
| **Vision** | Infravision |

**Description:** Massive, powerful beings from the swamp city of Oggok. Ogres have the highest Strength and Stamina in the game, making them exceptional Warriors and Shadowknights. Their most unique ability is Stun Immunity — unlike all other races, Ogres cannot be stunned while facing an enemy, which is enormously valuable for tanks and casters alike.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- Highest STR and STA in the game
- **Stun Immunity** — cannot be stunned while facing enemy (extremely powerful)
- Infravision
- Can play Warrior, Shadow Knight, Shaman, Beastlord
- Very large size — cannot fit through many dungeon doors

---

### ❤️ Troll

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Grobb |
| **Alignment** | Evil |
| **Size** | Large |
| **Vision** | Infravision |

**Description:** Fearsome, regenerating swamp creatures from Grobb. Trolls have very high Strength and Stamina plus an exceptional natural regeneration ability — the best out-of-combat HP regeneration in the game. This makes them resilient tanks that recover faster between fights than any other race. They pay for this with extremely low Intelligence and Charisma.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- ❤️ **Best natural regeneration** in the game
- High STR and STA
- Infravision
- Can play Warrior, Shadow Knight, Shaman, Beastlord
- Very low INT and CHA

---

### 🐱 Vah Shir

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Shar Vahl (Luclin expansion) |
| **Alignment** | Good, Neutral |
| **Size** | Large |
| **Vision** | Infravision |

**Description:** Feline humanoids from the moon of Luclin. Vah Shir are agile and strong, naturally suited to Monks, Beastlords, and Warriors. They have a built-in safe fall ability and begin with the Beastlord class available. Added in the Shadows of Luclin expansion.

**Racial Abilities/Bonuses:**
- Safe Fall ability (no fall damage reduction penalty)
- Sneak skill bonus
- Infravision
- High AGI
- Can play Warrior, Rogue, Shaman, Beastlord, Monk (in some eras)
- *Only available in Luclin and later expansions*

---

### 🧝 Wood Elf

| Attribute | Details |
|-----------|---------|
| **Starting City** | Kelethin (on platforms in Greater Faydark) |
| **Alignment** | Good |
| **Size** | Medium |
| **Vision** | Infravision |

**Description:** Lithe, agile forest elves who live in the treetop city of Kelethin in Greater Faydark. Wood Elves have high Agility and Dexterity, making them natural Rangers and Druids. ## 🌟 Beginner Recommendations

### 🏆 Top 3 Easiest Class/Race Combos for New Players

---

#### #1 — Magician (Gnome or Erudite)

**Why it works for beginners:**
- Your Earth elemental pet does the tanking — you stay at range and are rarely in danger
- You can summon your own food, water, and even weapons/armor (for the pet)
- No need to find a group in the early and mid-game; you are fully self-sufficient
- Simple gameplay: send pet in, nuke from range, med between fights
- If things go wrong, your pet gives you time to run
- Erudite gives the highest INT (more mana), Gnome gives Tinkering and Infravision

**What you'll learn:** Pet management, mana pacing, and basic camp mechanics — all fundamental EQ skills.

---

#### #2 — Druid (Human, Half Elf, or Halfling)

**Why it works for beginners:**
- Snare + Damage-over-Time (DoT) kiting is a safe, effective solo method at almost every level
- Can heal yourself in an emergency
- **Teleportation** makes you immediately useful to groups and lets you explore the world freely
- Very forgiving — Succor lets you escape dangerous situations as a last resort
- Strong demand in groups means you'll always find a party when you want one
- Outdoor tracking makes finding mobs and named spawns easy

**What you'll learn:** Kiting mechanics, mana management, group healing fundamentals, and EQ's geography (via ports).

---

#### #3 — Paladin (Dwarf or Human)

**Why it works for beginners:**
- Plate armor means you survive a lot of hits — very forgiving in early combat
- Lay on Hands (massive self-heal) is an emergency button that can save you once every 72 hours
- Can cast heals to patch yourself or help a group in an emergency
- Always welcome in groups — tanks are always needed
- Dwarf Paladin in particular has excellent stats (high WIS = more mana, high STA = more HP) and starts in Kaladim near good early hunting zones
- Undead-fighting abilities are useful in many classic dungeons (Befallen, Lower Guk, etc.)

**What you'll learn:** Aggro management, group dynamics, and the fundamentals of being a frontline fighter with backup utility.

---

### ❌ Classes to Avoid as a First Character

| Class | Why It's Tough for Beginners |
|-------|------------------------------|
| **Enchanter** | Failed crowd control = instant group wipe; extremely punishing |
| **Bard** | Song twisting requires fast, precise manual input every 3 seconds |
| **Warrior** | Basically helpless without a group; very little solo play |
| **Necromancer** | Evil race restrictions, complex mechanics, poor faction everywhere |
| **Iksar (any class)** | Faction issues make the entire non-Kunark world hostile |
