# ✨ Buffs & the Buff System in EverQuest

Buffs are temporary magical enhancements cast on you by yourself or other players. EQ's buff system is deep, meaningful, and central to how the game is played — especially at higher levels where some buffs are the difference between surviving a dungeon and dying in seconds. This guide explains how buffs work, which ones to seek out, and the culture around buffing.

---

## 🪄 How Buffs Work

### Buff Slots
Each character has a limited number of **buff slots** — the total number of buffs you can have active at once:
- **Classic / P1999:** 15 short-term buff slots + some song slots for Bards
- **EQLive (modern):** Up to 25+ buff slots depending on era/AA
- When all slots are full, new buffs **overwrite** old ones — usually the shortest-duration buff gets replaced

### Duration
Every buff has a duration — shown in your buff window (default: top-right corner of screen). Some buffs last:
- **Seconds** — combat procs, Bard songs (refresh constantly while Bard plays)
- **Minutes** — most combat buffs (haste, strength, AC)
- **Hours** — high-end buffs like Clarity II, Spirit of Wolf
- **Ticks** — EQ uses "ticks" (6-second intervals) as the base time unit internally

### Zones and Buffs
- Buffs **do NOT persist through zoning** in the classic era on some servers
- On EQLive, most buffs survive zoning
- On P1999: re-buff after long zones or dungeon changes

### Canceling a Buff
Right-click a buff icon in your buff window → **Remove Buff** if you need a slot for something better.

## 🌟 The Most Valuable Buffs to Seek Out

These are the buffs every player should know about and actively try to get before heading into serious content:

### 🐺 Spirit of the Wolf (SoW)
**Cast by:** Druids (level 14), Rangers (higher level version)
- **Effect:** +55% movement speed
- **Duration:** ~18–27 minutes (scales with caster level)
- **Why it matters:** EQ has no mounts at low/mid levels. Running across zones takes forever. SoW makes travel dramatically faster.
- **How to get it:** Ask a Druid in `/ooc`. Most will cast it for free or a small tip (5–20pp). It's one of the most-requested buffs in the game.

> 💡 "Can I get SoW please?" is one of the most-uttered sentences in EQ history.

### 💙 Clarity / Brilliance / Flowing Thought (Mana Regen)
**Cast by:** Enchanters
- **Clarity** (Enchanter level 18): Moderate mana regeneration
- **Clarity II** (Enchanter level 49): Strong mana regeneration
- **Brilliance** (Enchanter level 44+): Increases INT
- **Why it matters:** Casters naturally regenerate mana slowly. Clarity can double or triple your regen rate, meaning less downtime between pulls.
- **How to get it:** Ask an Enchanter. Many hang around in PoK (EQLive) or EC tunnel (P1999) and will buff passersby.

### ⚡ Haste (Attack Speed)
**Cast by:** Enchanters (Haste, Alacrity, Swift Like the Wind), Shamans (Quickness), Bards (Anthem De Arms)
- **Effect:** Increases attack speed — you swing weapons much faster
- **Why it matters:** For melee classes, haste is often more DPS than any weapon upgrade
- **How to get it:** Enchanters in groups usually have this. Also available from items (Haste items are very sought after)

### 🌿 Regeneration
**Cast by:** Druids (Regeneration line), Rangers
- **Effect:** Increases HP regeneration per tick
- **Why it matters:** Reduces downtime between fights, especially useful for melee who don't meditate
- Combined with sitting, Regen makes a huge difference in sustained combat efficiency

### 💪 Strength / Dexterity Buffs
**Cast by:** Clerics (Strength of Earth), Shamans (many options), Enchanters (Strengthen)
- Boosts STR for melee damage and carry weight
- Boosts DEX for procs and ranged accuracy

### 🛡️ AC / Armor Class Buffs
**Cast by:** Clerics (Armor of Faith), Druids, Shamans
- Increases your AC — reduces incoming damage
- Critical for tanks, useful for everyone

### 👁️ See Invisible / Infravision
**Cast by:** Druids, Rangers, Wizards, Enchanters, Shamans, Necromancers
- **See Invisible:** Lets you see invisible monsters — required in some dungeons
- **Infravision:** Lets you see in the dark — reduces need for torches or light items

### 🌬️ Levitate
**Cast by:** Druids, Wizards, Magicians, Necromancers (later levels), Clerics (later)
- **Effect:** You float — can cross lava, avoid fall damage, walk over certain terrain
- **Essential in:** Planes of Power (stepping off platforms kills you without lev), some dungeons

### 🔥 Symbol / Skin (HP + AC)
**Cast by:** Clerics
- **Symbol of Naltron / Transal / etc.:** Increases max HP significantly
- Very powerful pre-fight buff — significantly increases a tank's survivability

## 🏛️ Getting Buffed in PoK / EC Tunnel

A beloved EQ tradition is standing near popular gathering spots and asking for buffs:

### EQLive: Plane of Knowledge (PoK)
- Stand near the **stone spires** or main gathering area
- Type in `/ooc`: *"Looking for SoW and Clarity please, tipping!"*
- Many players buff for free; tipping 5–20pp per buff is good etiquette

### P1999: East Commonlands Tunnel
- The EC tunnel is both a trading hub AND a buffing spot
- Same process: ask in `/ooc` or `/shout`
- Druids and Enchanters often park there between sessions and will buff you

### Etiquette
- **Tip your buffers.** 5–20pp per major buff is the norm. High-level buffs (Clarity II, Symbol) deserve more.
- **Don't ask for more than a few buffs at once.** Don't roll up and ask for 10 different buffs without offering compensation.
- **Say thank you.** It's a player doing you a favor.

## 🎵 Bard Songs (Passive Buffs)

Bards work differently — they **play songs continuously** to buff the group:
- Songs only apply while the Bard is actively playing (cycling through their song bar)
- Effects include: movement speed, attack speed, mana regen, HP regen, resistance buffs, and more
- A good Bard is like having 2–3 Enchanters' worth of buffs running permanently
- **You don't do anything** to receive Bard songs — just be in the group

## ⚔️ Combat Buffs vs. Pre-Fight Buffs

### Pre-Fight Buffs (Cast Before Pulling)
Load up on these before engaging:
- Haste, Strength, Regen, SoW, AC buffs, Symbol (HP)
- These are long-duration and should be refreshed when they expire

### Combat Buffs (Cast During a Fight)
- **Heals** — not technically buffs, but reactive casting
- **Root / Snare** — movement debuffs on enemies
- **Slow** (Shaman: Turgur's Insects, etc.) — reduces enemy attack speed — **game-changing**

### Buffs to Get BEFORE a Dungeon Run
At minimum before entering a serious dungeon:
1. ✅ SoW (for travel to get there)
2. ✅ Haste (melee DPS boost)
3. ✅ Clarity (if you're a caster)
4. ✅ Symbol / HP buff (tanks especially)
5. ✅ See Invisible (if the dungeon has invis mobs — ask locals)
6. ✅ Levitate (if the dungeon needs it)

## 🔢 Buff Stacking Rules

Not all buffs stack with each other:
- **Two haste buffs** from the same spell line do NOT stack — only the strongest applies
- **SoW and another movement speed buff** — only one applies
- **HP buffs** generally stack as long as they're from different spell lines
- When in doubt, cast the higher-level version and the weaker one will be automatically overwritten or won't land

> 💡 If a buff "doesn't take" (no icon appears after being cast), it either didn't stack with something you already have, or all your buff slots are full.

## 💡 Buff Tips

> 🎒 **Free buffs are everywhere at low levels.** High-level players hanging out in newbie zones will often dump free buffs on you unprompted. Don't be surprised when a level 50 Druid runs by and SoWs you for free.

> ⏰ **Watch your buff timers.** When SoW or Clarity is about to expire (shown in your buff window), you'll want to get them refreshed before a serious pull — especially in dungeons.

> 🚫 **Don't accept buffs you don't want.** If someone cast something in a slot you needed, you can right-click it off. Politely let them know if they're filling your slots with low-value buffs.

> 💊 **Potions are portable buffs.** The bazaar (EQLive) and some vendors sell potion versions of common buffs. Useful when no player is available to cast the real thing.
