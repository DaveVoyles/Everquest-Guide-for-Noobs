# 💰 Economy, Trading & Making Plat in EverQuest

EverQuest has no auction house. No global market interface. No "scan" button to find the cheapest price. Instead, it has a **completely player-driven economy** that's raw, social, and surprisingly deep once you understand it. This guide explains how money works, how to make more of it, and how to buy and sell effectively.

---

## 🪙 Currency

EQ uses four currency denominations:

| Currency | Abbreviation | Conversion |
|----------|-------------|------------|
| Copper | **cp** | Base unit |
| Silver | **sp** | 1 sp = 10 cp |
| Gold | **gp** | 1 gp = 10 sp = 100 cp |
| Platinum | **pp** | 1 pp = 10 gp = 100 sp = 1,000 cp |

**Platinum is what matters.** Almost everything meaningful is priced in pp. Copper and silver are effectively vendor trash values.

---

## 🛒 Where the Economy Happens

### EQLive: The Bazaar
The **Bazaar** (accessible via the Guild Lobby or Plane of Knowledge) is EQLive's player market:
- Set up a **trader** — go offline in trader mode and your character sells automatically while you're away
- Browse sellers with the Bazaar search window
- Much more convenient than the old EC tunnel system

### P1999 / Classic: The East Commonlands Tunnel
Before the Bazaar existed, the **East Commonlands (EC) tunnel** was THE trading hub on most servers:
- Players stand in the tunnel and **spam `/auction`** with what they're selling or buying
- To sell: `/auction WTS [Item Name] Xpp` (WTS = Want To Sell)
- To buy: `/auction WTB [Item Name]` (WTB = Want To Buy)
- Prices are negotiated in `/tell`
- Best times: peak server hours — the tunnel gets genuinely crowded

> 💡 **EC tunnel tip (P1999):** The tunnel is in the **western portion** of East Commonlands. Run to where you see other players standing and start shouting. It's chaotic but it works.

### The Plane of Knowledge (EQLive)
PoK has **NPC vendors for everything** and is a hub where players trade and buff each other. Great for buying spells and basic gear.

## 💸 Making Platinum: The Basics

### 1. Vendor Trash Everything
Every monster drops some combination of:
- **Coin** (auto-collected)
- **Items** — some are worth selling to players, most go to vendors

Get in the habit of **vendoring** after every camp session. Even low-value drops add up.

### 2. Know What's Worth Keeping vs. Vendoring

| Item Type | What to Do |
|-----------|-----------|
| Coin | Auto-looted — keep it all |
| Common armor/weapons | Vendor, usually worth 1–20 pp |
| Rare named mob drops | **Check the wiki before vendoring!** Could be 100–1000+ pp |
| Tradeskill components | Sell to players — often worth far more than vendor price |
| Spells (scrolls) | Check if any class needs them — sell to players |
| MAGIC items your class can't use | Sell to players who can |
| NO DROP items you don't need | Check if groupmates want them; can't sell otherwise |

> ⚠️ **Never vendor a rare drop without looking it up first.** A mob named "a shadowed man" might drop something worth 500pp that you'd vendor for 2pp. This is how players accidentally throw away their best early money.

### 3. Farm High-Value Drops

Some zones are worth farming specifically for sellable items:

| Zone | Level Range | What to Farm |
|------|------------|-------------|
| Blackburrow | 5–15 | Gnoll Fangs (quest item, sell to players) |
| East Commonlands | 5–20 | Banded armor pieces (sell to players) |
| Unrest | 15–25 | Gems, good drops — sell at EC tunnel |
| Mistmoore | 20–35 | Gems, teeth, quality drops for selling |
| Lower Guk | 35–50 | High-value tradeable gear — very profitable |
| Velious zones | 45–60 | Endgame gear with strong market value |

### 4. Sell Tradeskill Components

Players who level tradeskills buy enormous quantities of:
- **Bat Wings**, **Snake Scales**, **Animal Pelts** — from common mobs
- **Silk** (from spiders), **Bone Chips** (skeletons), **Ruined Pelts**
- **High Quality (HQ) Pelts** — worth significantly more than low quality versions

> 💡 Check what's selling in EC tunnel or the Bazaar before leaving a camp. If you killed 40 spiders, that silk might be worth more to a tailor than to a vendor.

### 5. Help Players With Buffs (Casters)
If you're a caster with useful buffs:
- **Druids/Bards:** Spirit of the Wolf (SoW) — players will pay 5–20pp for this
- **Enchanters:** Clarity/Breeze (mana regen) — high demand, tip well
- **Shamans:** Slow, Haste — extremely valuable in groups, can command tips
- **Wizards:** Port — people pay for ports regularly

Stand near a city/zone entrance and offer buffs. You can make good plat per hour this way doing almost nothing.

### 6. Tradeskill Profits (Intermediate)

Some tradeskills produce items that sell well:
- **Brewing:** Alcohol sells to players; some brewed items are useful
- **Baking:** Food items with stat bonuses sell
- **Tailoring/Smithing:** High-level armor patterns sell well if you have the skills
- **Research:** Researched spells can sell for hundreds of pp if they're rare

Tradeskills require upfront investment, but a skilled crafter can turn a profit long-term.

## 🤝 How to Buy From Players

### On EQLive (Bazaar)
1. Go to the Bazaar
2. Open the **Trader Search** window
3. Search by item name, type, or stat
4. Click "Buy" — the transaction is automatic

### On P1999 / Classic (EC Tunnel)
1. Watch `/auction` in your chat for people selling what you want
2. Send them a `/tell`: *"Hi, is [item] still available?"*
3. Negotiate price if needed — prices are suggestions, not fixed
4. Meet up in the tunnel and **trade** (right-click the player → Trade)
5. Both parties see the trade window — verify items and coin before clicking Accept

> ⚠️ **Scam awareness:** Always double-check the trade window before clicking Accept. Common scam: substitute a similar-looking but worthless item. Verify the item NAME carefully.

## 💬 Trading Lingo

These abbreviations show up constantly in `/auction`:

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **WTS** | Want to Sell |
| **WTB** | Want to Buy |
| **WTT** | Want to Trade |
| **PST** | Please Send Tell (contact me) |
| **OBO** | Or Best Offer |
| **LTS** | Looking to Sell (same as WTS) |
| **PC** | Price Check — asking what something is worth |
| **MQ** | Multi-Quest — splitting a multi-part quest reward between characters |
| **Link** | Item link in chat (EQLive: Shift+click item in inventory) |

### Sending an Auction
```
/auction WTS [Shining Brass Shield] 50pp OBO PST
```
```
/auction WTB [Flowing Black Silk Sash] paying well PST
```

## 💡 Economy Tips

> 🔍 **Price check before selling.** Type `/auction PC [Item Name] PST` and see what people say. Veteran players often respond with current market value.

> 📈 **Buy low, sell high.** Some players sell items far under market price because they need fast cash. If you know values, you can buy cheap and resell in the tunnel for profit.

> 🎒 **Stack your coin.** If you're carrying loose copper and silver, visit a bank and consolidate. Large platinum stacks take the same inventory space as a single coin.

> 🏦 **Use the bank.** Every major city has a **Banker NPC**. Store valuables and excess coin there — your bank bag slots are separate from your inventory. You can't lose banked items on death.

> ⏳ **Patience pays.** The right buyer for a rare item might not be online right now. List it repeatedly over several sessions rather than dropping the price immediately.

> 💎 **Gems from kills** are almost always vendor fodder, but a few have tradeskill uses. Look up gems before vendoring if you've never seen the type before.
