# 🗺️ Travel & Zones in EverQuest

EverQuest's world of Norrath is **vast, seamless, and genuinely dangerous to traverse**. There are no flying mounts, no teleport stones on every corner, and no auto-path to follow. Getting from point A to point B is a meaningful part of the game. This guide explains how travel works, the tools available to you, and how to navigate safely.

---

## 🌍 How the World Is Structured

Norrath is divided into **distinct zones** — separate maps connected by invisible zone lines. When you cross a zone line:
- A brief loading screen appears
- You appear at the entrance of the next zone
- Any mobs chasing you **stop at the zone line** (this is your escape route!)

### Zone Line Locations
Zone lines are usually:
- At the **edge of a map** in obvious geographic locations (mountain passes, cave entrances, coastlines)
- Marked on community maps (see the [Maps & Navigation guide](maps.md))
- Often guarded by tough mobs — be careful near zone lines in dangerous areas

## 🏃 Basic Movement

| Key | Action |
|-----|--------|
| **W / ↑** | Move forward |
| **S / ↓** | Move backward |
| **A / ←** | Turn left |
| **D / →** | Turn right |
| **Num Lock** | Toggle **auto-run** (hands-free forward movement) |
| **Page Up** | Look up |
| **Page Down** | Look down |
| **Space** | Jump |

> 💡 **Auto-run (Num Lock) is essential for long travel.** Press it once and you'll run forward until you press it again or a key. Use it for cross-continent runs while you watch TV or chat.

## 🐺 Movement Speed Buffs

Default run speed in EQ feels slow. These spells dramatically help:

### Spirit of the Wolf (SoW)
- **Cast by:** Druids (level 14), Rangers
- **Effect:** +55% movement speed for ~18–27 minutes
- **How to get it:** Ask a Druid in `/ooc` — it's the most-requested buff in the game
- This single buff transforms cross-zone travel from annoying to manageable

### Jboots (Journeyman's Boots)
- A **permanent item** that provides run speed similar to SoW
- Obtained through a long quest (see [Quests guide](quests.md))
- One of the most sought-after items in the classic era

### Other Speed Items/Spells
- **Speed of the Shissar** (higher level SoW equivalent)
- Various **horse mounts** (EQLive, later eras)
- **Bard songs** — Bards provide a group speed song passively

## ✈️ Teleportation: The Fast Travel System

Several classes provide fast travel through teleportation spells:

### Druid: Translocate / Succor / Evacuation
**Succor / Evacuation (Druid, Wizard):**
- Emergency escape spell
- Teleports the caster (and nearby group members) to a safe location in the current zone
- Not a long-distance teleport — it moves you to the "safe spot" of the zone
- Lifesaver in a dungeon wipe — caster says "Evac!" and everyone runs to them

**Druid Teleport Spells:**
- Druids get spells to teleport themselves (and a group) to specific outdoor zones
- **Common Druid ports:** Commonlands, Karana, Faydwer, Antonica, Cabilis areas
- Requires the Druid to have the appropriate spell memorized

### Wizard: Teleport / Gate
**Wizard Teleport Spells:**
- Wizards can teleport groups to specific locations
- **Wider range** than Druids — Wizards can reach some zones Druids cannot
- **Wizard Spires** — specific in-zone objects Wizards use for certain teleports

**Common port destinations:**
- Nexus (Luclin hub)
- Plane of Knowledge (EQLive hub)
- Various city/zone entrances across Norrath

### How to Get a Port
1. Find a Druid or Wizard in the zone (check `/who all druid` or `/who all wizard`)
2. Send a polite `/tell`: *"Hi! Can I get a port to [destination]? Happy to tip."*
3. Meet up and they cast the teleport on you
4. Tipping 10–50pp is standard depending on the distance — always offer

### Translocator NPCs (EQLive)
EQLive has **NPC translocators** in many cities and the Plane of Knowledge that offer teleports for a small plat fee — no player Druid/Wizard required.

## 🚢 Boats and Travel Routes

In the classic era, some intercontinental travel requires **boats**:

### The Antonica ↔ Faydwer Route
- **Freeport → Butcherblock:** Take the boat from East Freeport docks
- Boat runs on a schedule — wait at the dock and it arrives eventually
- Ride takes a few minutes in-game time

### The Faydwer ↔ Odus Route
- **Butcherblock → Erudin:** Boat from Butcherblock docks to the Erudite island

### Important: Boats Can Be Dangerous
- Mobs can sometimes path onto boats in classic era
- **Don't AFK on a boat** in dangerous water zones — sea creatures can aggro

### Boat Timing Tip
Boats run on a fixed schedule. If you just missed one, it typically returns in a few minutes. Wait at the dock — don't wander off or you'll miss it.

## 🌉 Key Travel Hubs

These zones are central to navigation in Norrath:

### The Commons (West and East Commonlands)
- Central hub connecting Freeport to the rest of the continent
- EC tunnel is the trading hub on classic servers
- Many travel routes pass through here

### Greater Faydark
- The hub of Faydwer continent
- Connects to Kelethin, Crushbone, Lesser Faydark, Butcherblock
- Large zone — easy to get lost in the forest

### Plane of Knowledge (PoK) — EQLive
- The **ultimate travel hub** in EQLive
- Has **books** that teleport you to every major zone in the game
- Connects to all hubs on all continents
- Has vendors, trainers, and banks all in one place

### The Nexus (Luclin era)
- Hub for the Luclin moon
- Has spires connecting to Antonica, Faydwer, and other landmasses

## 🧭 Orientation: Finding Your Way

EQ's maps range from sparse to nonexistent depending on the era and zone.

### Coordinates
```
/loc
```
Shows your current **X, Y, Z coordinates**. These correspond to positions on zone maps. When someone says "meet me at -300, 1200" they're giving map coordinates.

### Sense Heading
The **Sense Heading** skill (press **H** by default) tells you which direction you're facing: North, South, East, West, NE, SW, etc. Higher skill = more accurate reading. Raise it by using it constantly.

### Zone Maps
- Install **Brewall's map pack** for detailed community maps (see [Maps & Navigation guide](maps.md))
- Use **Ctrl+M** to open your in-game map

## ⚠️ Dangerous Travel Zones

Some zones are genuinely lethal to cross at the wrong level:

| Zone | Danger | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| **Kithicor Forest** | 🔴 Very High (at night) | Spawns high-level undead at night — zone is deadly after sundown |
| **Oasis of Marr** | 🟡 Moderate | Giants roam near the oasis and aggro from far away |
| **Ocean of Tears** | 🟡 Moderate | Large zone, sea creatures |
| **Eastern Wastes** (Velious) | 🔴 Very High | High-level mobs everywhere |
| **Permafrost** entrance | 🟡 Moderate | Cold mobs with large aggro radius near entrance |

> ⚠️ **Kithicor at night is infamous.** Many new players have been destroyed walking through Kithicor after dark without knowing the zone transforms. If you must cross, go during daytime game hours or get a port around it.

## 🗺️ Continent Overview

### Antonica (Starting Continent)
Home to: Qeynos, Freeport, many starting zones
Key zones: West/East Commonlands, Karanas, Lavastorm, Solusek's Eye

### Faydwer
Home to: Kelethin, Kaladim, Felwithe, Ak'Anon
Key zones: Greater/Lesser Faydark, Crushbone, Butcherblock

### Odus
Home to: Erudin, Paineel
Smallest continent; connected by boat

### Kunark (1st expansion)
Home to: Cabilis (Iksar city), Field of Bone, Sebilis
Key zones: Karnor's Castle, Old Sebilis, Chardok — endgame content

### Velious (2nd expansion)
Home to: Dwarves, Dragons, Giants
Key zones: Great Divide, Eastern Wastes, Temple of Veeshan, Ntov — raid content

### Luclin (3rd expansion)
Home to: Vah Shir (cat people), various moon zones
Accessed via **spires** or wizard teleports
