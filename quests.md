# 📜 Quests in EverQuest

EQ's quest system is nothing like modern MMOs. There's no glowing exclamation mark over NPCs, no quest tracker, no compass arrow pointing you to the next objective. Quests are discovered by **talking to NPCs**, reading their dialogue carefully, and piecing together what they need. This guide explains how quests work and points you toward the most important ones.

---

## 🤝 How Quests Work

### Finding Quests
- **Hail an NPC** (`/hail <name>` or just left-click while targeting) to start a conversation
- NPCs often give hints about quests in their greeting text
- **Keywords** in NPC dialogue appear in a different color — say those words back to continue
- Example: An NPC says *"I've been having trouble with the [gnolls]."* Type `/say gnolls` to continue the conversation

### Responding to NPCs
```
/say <keyword>
```
Or just type the keyword in your chat bar without the slash. Many players just type keywords directly.

### Quest Journals
EQ has **no automatic quest tracker** in the classic era. Your options:
- **Read carefully** and take notes
- **Use the in-game notebook** (some versions) to jot down objectives
- **Rely on external resources** — Allakhazam, the P1999 wiki, and EQ Resource have full quest walkthroughs

> 💡 **External resources are essential for quests.** EQ's quests were never designed to be self-documenting. Every serious player uses a wiki.

## 📦 Quest Turn-Ins

Most quests require you to **give items to an NPC**:

1. Target the quest NPC
2. Right-click them → **Trade**
3. Place the required items in the trade window
4. Click **Trade** or **Give**
5. The NPC takes the items and rewards you

> ⚠️ **Be careful with turn-ins!** The NPC takes your items the moment you click Give/Trade. If you give the wrong item, it may be gone. Always double-check before confirming.

### Give Command
Some older quests use the `/give` command while items are in your cursor. But the Trade window method is safer and more reliable.

## 🏆 Newbie / Starter Quests

Every starting city has **newbie quests** — short tasks that reward class-appropriate gear and help you learn your area. These are some of the best gear you'll see at levels 1–10.

### How to Find Them
- Hail NPCs near your starting point, especially **guards, guildmasters, and named citizens**
- Ask in `/say` "I am looking for work" or "what can I do to help?"
- Common reward types: weapons, armor pieces, bags, food

### Examples by City

| City | Quest | Reward |
|------|-------|--------|
| Qeynos | Gnoll Head Quest (bring scalps to guards) | Faction + coin |
| Freeport | Various militia/citizen tasks | Armor pieces |
| Halas | Barbarian heritage quests | Weapons |
| Kelethin | Wood Elf starting quests | Leather armor |
| Oggok | Ogre starting tasks | Weapons |

## 🔑 Key Quests to Know About

### Soulfire (Paladin) / Ghoulbane (Paladin/Shadow Knight)
Long quest chains rewarding iconic weapons with undead-slaying properties. Worth doing as these classes hit the right level range.

### The Journeyman's Boots
One of EQ's most famous items — provides permanent run speed increase (essentially free SoW). Obtainable through a long quest chain involving the Coldain in Velious. A major quality-of-life upgrade.

### Epic Weapons (Level 45–60)
**Epic weapon quests** are the pinnacle of EQ's quest system — long, server-spanning journeys that require:
- Multiple dungeon runs
- Faction grinding
- Rare item drops
- Player cooperation (some steps require groups or raids)

Each class has its own **1.0 Epic** weapon — a unique, powerful item that defines the character. Epics are a major goal for level 45–60 players.

| Class | Epic Weapon |
|-------|-------------|
| Warrior | Jagged Blade of War |
| Cleric | Water Sprinkler of Nem Ankh |
| Wizard | Staff of the Four |
| Necromancer | Scythe of the Shadowed Soul |
| Enchanter | Twisted Staff of the Ancients |
| Magician | Staff of the Four (variant) |
| Druid | Nature Walker's Scimitar |

> 💡 Epic quests are extremely involved — budget multiple weeks or months. But completing your Epic is one of the most satisfying experiences in EQ.

## 🌟 The Planes Keys (Classic Era)

Accessing certain high-end zones requires **keys** obtained through quests:

### Plane of Fear
No key required to enter, but staying alive requires raid-level preparation.

### Plane of Hate
Requires the **Fuligan Soulstone of Innoruuk** — an item that grants entry. Obtained through rare drops or player trade.

### Plane of Sky
Requires being **brought in by a raiding guild** with access — this plane is island-by-island with strict access control.

### Plane of Growth / Plane of Mischief (Velious)
Various access quests involving Velious faction.

## 🗝️ Zone Access Quests

Many dungeons and premium zones require specific **flagging** (proving you've done prerequisite content):

| Zone | Access Requirement |
|------|-------------------|
| **Veeshan's Peak** (classic) | The Key to Veeshan's Peak — very long quest |
| **Seb Key** (Sebilis) | Obtain from quest in Trakanon's Teeth |
| **Chardok** | Key obtained from a lengthy faction quest |
| **City of Mist** | Key obtained from Burning Woods |
| **Planes of Power zones** (EQLive) | Flagging progression through earlier planes |

These keys are often major mid-game goals. The EQ community refers to the process as "getting keyed."

## 💡 Quest Tips

> 🗣️ **Hail every NPC you see.** Many quest-givers don't advertise themselves. The innkeeper who says "Greetings, traveler" might start a 20-step quest chain if you say the right keywords.

> 📝 **Write things down.** Seriously. EQ's quest dialogue can be cryptic. Keep a notepad or text file open while questing and copy the key info before you close the conversation.

> 🔍 **Allakhazam comments are gold.** The player-submitted comments on Allakhazam often have exact dialogue trees, corrections to quest bugs, and tips not in the official description.

> ⏱️ **Some quests have timers.** A few quests require you to deliver items within a time limit after receiving them. The NPC will hint at urgency in dialogue.

> 🤝 **Ask for help.** Quest steps that require group content (dungeon runs, rare drops) are done by the community regularly. Post in `/ooc` or your guild chat — many veteran players help with epic quests just for the fun of it.
