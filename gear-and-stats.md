# ⚔️ Gear, Stats & Equipment in EverQuest

Gear matters enormously in EQ. Unlike modern MMOs that automatically equip items and explain every stat, EQ drops items in your inventory and leaves you to figure out whether they're good. This guide explains every stat, which ones matter for your class, and how to get decent gear as a new player.

---

## 🎒 Equipment Slots

Your character has **21 equipment slots**:

| Slot | What Goes There |
|------|----------------|
| 👑 Head | Helms, hats, hoods |
| 👂 Ear (x2) | Earrings |
| 👁️ Face | Masks, eyepatches |
| 🔮 Neck | Necklaces, amulets |
| 💪 Shoulders | Pauldrons, mantles |
| 🛡️ Arms | Vambraces, sleeves |
| 🖐️ Wrist (x2) | Bracer, armband |
| 🧤 Hands | Gauntlets, gloves |
| 🫀 Chest | Breastplate, robe, tunic |
| 🩲 Legs | Greaves, leggings |
| 👟 Feet | Boots, sandals |
| 💍 Finger (x2) | Rings |
| 🎒 Back | Cloaks, packs |
| 🧳 Waist | Belts, girdles |
| 🗡️ Primary | Main-hand weapon |
| 🛡️ Secondary | Off-hand weapon, shield, or secondary |
| 🏹 Range | Ranged weapon (bow, thrown) |
| 🎵 Ammo | Arrows, bolts, thrown items |

To equip an item: **right-click it** in your inventory, or drag it to the appropriate slot in your character window (**C** to open).

## 📊 Stats Explained

EQ has many stats. Here's what each does and who cares about it:

### 💪 Strength (STR)
- Increases **melee damage** and **carry weight**
- Important for: Warriors, Paladins, Shadow Knights, Rangers, Bards
- Less important for: Pure casters (Wizards, Enchanters, etc.)

### 🏃 Stamina (STA)
- Increases your **maximum HP** (health points)
- **Everyone benefits from this** — more HP = harder to die
- Most important for: Tanks (Warriors, Paladins, Shadow Knights), but every class wants it

### 🐱 Agility (AGI)
- Increases your **Armor Class (AC)** slightly and **avoidance**
- Affects how often you dodge attacks
- Important for: All melee, especially tanks and Monks
- Diminishing returns at high values in classic era

### 🎯 Dexterity (DEX)
- Affects **chance to hit**, **proc rate** (weapon special effects), and some ranged attacks
- Important for: Rogues (backstab), Rangers (archery), Warriors (procs)
- Moderate value for other melee

### 🧠 Intelligence (INT)
- Increases **maximum mana** for INT-based casters
- Important for: Wizards, Necromancers, Enchanters, Magicians, Shadow Knights
- **Useless** for WIS-based casters and melee

### 🌿 Wisdom (WIS)
- Increases **maximum mana** for WIS-based casters
- Important for: Clerics, Druids, Shamans, Rangers, Paladins, Beastlords
- **Useless** for INT-based casters and pure melee

### 🎭 Charisma (CHA)
- Affects **vendor prices** (higher CHA = cheaper buys/better sells)
- Affects **Enchanter spell effectiveness** (Charm, Mesmerize success rate)
- Also affects some faction interactions
- Important for: Enchanters (critical), merchants (situationally), everyone else (minor)

### ❤️ HP (Hit Points)
- Your life total — when it hits 0, you die
- Boosted directly by items with `+HP` and indirectly by Stamina
- More is always better for everyone

### 💙 Mana
- Your spell resource — casters spend it to cast spells
- Boosted directly by items with `+Mana` and indirectly by INT or WIS
- Non-casters have mana but rarely use it

### 🛡️ AC (Armor Class)
- Reduces physical damage taken per hit
- Affected by your equipped armor and AGI
- Most important for: Tanks — Warriors especially want maximum AC

## 🔍 Identifying Items

When you loot an item, it may appear as **"?" with unknown stats**. To find out what it does:

### Option 1: Use an Identify Scroll
- Buy or loot a **Spell: Identify** scroll (Wizards and some classes can cast this)
- Right-click the scroll and target the item

### Option 2: Ask an Enchanter
Enchanters have the **Identify** spell. Ask nicely in `/ooc` — most will help for free.

### Option 3: Look It Up
- **Allakhazam** (allakhazam.com) — the classic EQ item database
- **P1999 wiki** (wiki.project1999.com) — for classic era items
- **EQ Resource** (eqresource.com) — for EQLive

> 💡 **Don't vendor unknown items immediately.** That "?" item might be worth hundreds of plat to the right class. Identify first, then decide.

## 🛍️ Where to Get Starter Gear

### From Vendors (Cheap & Easy)
Every city has vendors selling basic armor for your class. It's not great, but it's better than nothing:
- **Melee:** Buy the heaviest armor your class can wear (Plate > Chain > Leather > Cloth)
- **Casters:** Focus on items with INT or WIS bonuses over raw AC

### From Quests
Your starting city has **newbie quests** that reward class-appropriate gear. These are often the best items you'll see at levels 1–10:
- Hail NPCs in your starting area and ask about work
- Common newbie quest rewards: tunics, weapons, boots with decent stats

### From Drops
Monsters drop gear. Some zones are specifically farmed for their drops:
- **Blackburrow** (levels 5–15): Gnoll hides, fangs, decent caster items
- **Unrest** (levels 15–25): Strong drops for this level range
- **Mistmoore** (levels 20–35): Popular farming zone, good drops

### From Other Players
The best beginner gear often comes from generous higher-level players or the player economy:
- Ask in `/ooc` if anyone has twink gear (hand-me-downs)
- Check the **bazaar** (EQLive) or **EC tunnel** (P1999) for affordable upgrades
- Players often give away gear that's an upgrade for you but worthless to them

## 🏷️ Item Flags to Know

| Flag | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| **LORE** | You can only have one of this item. Can't carry a second. |
| **NO DROP** | Cannot be traded or given to another player |
| **NO RENT** | Disappears when you log out — use immediately or discard |
| **MAGIC** | Required to hit certain monsters (skeletons, some undead) |
| **TEMPORARY** | Same as NO RENT |
| **QUEST** | Quest item — needed to turn in for a reward |
| **Recommended Level** | You CAN equip it below this level, but stats may be reduced |

> ⚠️ **MAGIC weapons matter.** Some monsters, especially undead and certain creatures, can only be hit by MAGIC weapons. If your attacks are doing 0 damage, check if you need a magic weapon.

## 💡 Gear Tips for New Players

> 🎒 **Bags are gear too.** Your inventory has 8 bag slots. Bigger bags = more loot capacity. Buy the largest bags you can afford early — 10-slot or 12-slot bags make a huge quality-of-life difference.

> 🔄 **Upgrade weapons before armor.** A better weapon dramatically improves your damage output. Better armor improves survivability but the difference at low levels is smaller.

> 💙 **Casters: prioritize INT/WIS items over AC.** Your mana pool is your lifeblood. An extra 10 INT is often worth more than 20 extra AC.

> 🛡️ **Tanks: AC and STA above all.** You're absorbing hits for the group. Stats that help you survive outlast stats that help you deal damage.

> 💰 **Don't sell loot too fast.** Some dropped items are tradeskill components worth more than vendor price if sold to other players.
