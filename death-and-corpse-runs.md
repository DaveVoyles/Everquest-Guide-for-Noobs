# ☠️ Death & Corpse Runs in EverQuest

Death in EverQuest is one of the most punishing — and memorable — experiences in any MMO. You don't respawn with your gear. You don't get a checkpoint. You wake up naked at your bind point, possibly miles away from where you died, with a chunk of your XP gone and all your gear sitting on a decaying corpse in a dangerous dungeon. This guide tells you how to handle it.

---

## 💀 What Happens When You Die

The moment your HP hits zero:

1. **You die** — your character collapses
2. **Your corpse appears** exactly where you fell, containing **all your equipped gear and inventory items**
3. **You lose a percentage of your current XP** — enough to de-level if you're unlucky
4. **You respawn at your bind point** — wearing nothing but your money (coin stays with you)
5. **A death message** appears in zone chat (on some servers): *"[YourName] has been slain by [Mob Name]!"*

> ⚠️ **You keep your coin.** Platinum, gold, silver, copper stays in your pocket when you die. Everything else is on the corpse.

---

## 📍 The Bind Point System

Your **bind point** is where you respawn after death. By default, you're bound to your **starting city**. This is fine for your first few deaths, but becomes a nightmare if you're leveling far from home.

### Setting Your Bind Point
```
/bind
```
Type this while standing somewhere safe. You're now bound here.

> ✅ **Rebind frequently** as you move to new areas. Bind at zone entrances near your current camp, in cities, or at inns. Never be more than a 5-minute run from your bind point when doing risky content.

### Who Can Bind You?
- **All classes** can bind themselves anywhere outdoors using `/bind`
- Some zones (dungeons, planes) **don't allow self-binding** — you need a Druid or Wizard to cast Bind Affinity on you
- **Druids** (level 14+) and **Wizards** can cast **Bind Affinity** on other players, binding them wherever they stand
- Getting a Druid to bind you at a dungeon entrance before a session is a common practice

## 🏃 The Corpse Run

A **corpse run** is the act of getting back to your corpse to retrieve your gear. It's one of EQ's most defining experiences.

### The Naked Run
After spawning at your bind point:
1. **Don't panic.** Your corpse will last for hours on most servers.
2. **Assess the situation.** How far away did you die? How dangerous is the route back?
3. **Run back naked** — you have no gear, which means you're very weak. Be extra cautious.
4. **Avoid mobs.** Sneak through, zone-hop around dangerous areas, or ask for an escort.
5. **Reach your corpse** and right-click to loot your gear back.

> 💡 On P1999, player corpses can persist for **up to 7 days**. You have time. On EQLive, the timer is shorter but still generous for most situations.

### Tips for a Successful Corpse Run
- **Ask for help.** Tell your group or `/ooc` that you died and need a CR (corpse run). Many players will assist.
- **Look for a Druid or Wizard** who can give you Spirit of the Wolf (SoW) for the run back.
- **Map the route mentally** before you go — plan which zones to run through and where the danger is.
- **Stay near zone lines.** If you get attacked while running naked, you can zone out and escape.
- **Travel in a group if possible.** Even one other player dramatically improves your chances.

## 🧟 Getting Your Corpse Moved or Summoned

Sometimes your corpse is in an impossible place — the middle of a boss room, deep in a dungeon with aggressive mobs between you and it. You have options:

### Corpse Dragging (Any Player)
Any player standing next to your corpse can drag it:
```
/corpse
```
This command, typed while standing next to someone's corpse, moves it with you. Players will drag corpses to safe spots (dungeon entrance, zone line) to help with recovery.

> 💡 **Ask nicely in `/ooc`**: *"CR request — died in Guk lower, anyone able to drag my corpse to zone entrance?"* This is a common, respected request.

### Corpse Summoning (Necromancer / Shadow Knight)
High-level **Necromancers** have spells that summon a player's corpse directly to them:
- Works from anywhere in the same zone
- Requires the Necromancer to be near or in the zone
- A major reason why Necromancers are beloved in EQ — they can rescue corpses from impossible positions

On EQLive, there is also a **Resurrection** category of spells that brings the player back with some XP restored (see below).

### GM Assistance (Last Resort)
If your corpse is unreachable and decaying, you can petition a **Game Master (GM)**:
- Type `/petition` to open a help request
- Explain what happened and where your corpse is
- GMs can restore corpses in legitimate cases — but this is not guaranteed and can take time
- Use this as a **last resort**, not a first option

## 💔 XP Loss on Death

Death causes **XP loss** — a percentage of your current level's progress. The exact amount varies by server and era:

| Server Type | XP Loss Per Death |
|-------------|------------------|
| Classic / P1999 | ~7–10% of current level's XP |
| EQLive (current) | Reduced — varies by era and AA spent |
| TLP servers | Varies by ruleset |

### Can You De-Level?
**Yes.** If you die with less XP than the death penalty costs, you will drop back to the previous level. This is genuinely awful at level 49 (one level before a major milestone). It happens. Take it in stride.

### XP Debt
Some eras of EQ introduced an **XP debt** system instead of direct loss — you essentially "owe" XP before you can gain again. EQLive has moved away from harsh death penalties over the years.

## ✨ Resurrection Spells

Certain classes can **resurrect** dead players, restoring some or all of their lost XP:

| Class | Spell | XP Restored |
|-------|-------|------------|
| Cleric | Resurrection (level 20) | 75% of lost XP |
| Cleric | Restoration (level 44) | 90% of lost XP |
| Cleric | Reanimation / True Resurrection | 96–100% of lost XP |
| Paladin | Revive (higher levels) | 50–75% of lost XP |
| Druid | Reincarnation | 75% of lost XP |
| Necromancer | Restore / Reviviscence | Partial |

### How Rez Works
1. You're dead (your corpse is on the ground)
2. The Cleric targets your corpse and casts the Resurrection spell
3. A **resurrection prompt** appears on your screen
4. You click **Accept** — you're revived at your corpse with some XP restored
5. You're alive again with your gear right where it fell — no naked corpse run needed

> 💡 **Always ask for a rez before doing a corpse run.** A Cleric rez saves you the run AND restores most of your XP. It's worth waiting a few minutes for.

## 🔮 Feign Death (Monks & Necromancers)

**Feign Death (FD)** lets these classes fake their own death to drop all aggro. This is NOT actually dying:
- All mobs that were attacking you **lose aggro** and walk away
- You're lying on the ground, appearing dead
- Stand back up when the coast is clear
- **Monks** have FD as a combat ability; **Necromancers** have it as a spell

FD is also used for **corpse recovery** — a Monk can FD, wait for pathing mobs to move, then stand up next to a corpse and drag it to safety.

## 🛡️ How to Reduce Your Death Risk

### Always Be Aware of Your HP
Watch your health bar. When it drops below 30%, start planning your escape route — don't wait until you're at 5%.

### Know Your Escape Route
Before each camp session, identify:
- Where the **zone line** is
- Which direction is **relative safety**
- Whether you can **run faster than the mob** (most outdoor mobs you can outrun)

### Have Gate Ready
If your class has **Gate** (Druid, Wizard, Cleric, Necromancer, Enchanter, Shadowknight, Paladin), keep it memorized at all times. Gate teleports you instantly to your bind point — it's your emergency escape.

```
Bind point near the dungeon entrance + Gate memorized = near-zero death penalty
```

### Don't Fight in Low-HP / Low-Mana States
Tired groups wipe more than rested ones. If the healer is at 10% mana, **sit and recover** before the next pull. The 60 seconds you spend recovering is worth far more than the 60 seconds you'd spend doing a corpse run.

### Watch Your /Con
**Always** `/con` mobs before engaging. A red con can kill you in 2–3 hits. Never pull what you can't survive.

---

## 😤 The Emotional Reality of Dying in EQ

Death in EQ feels bad in a way that modern MMO deaths don't. That's intentional. Some advice:

- **Breathe.** The corpse will be there. Your gear isn't gone yet.
- **Ask for help.** The community largely responds well to genuine distress.
- **Learn from it.** Every death teaches you something — too aggressive a pull, wrong camp spot, forgot to rebind.
- **Don't take it personally.** Sometimes you die to a random pat (patrolling mob) through no fault of your own. It happens.

> *"The only players who never die in EQ are the ones who never leave the starting city."*
