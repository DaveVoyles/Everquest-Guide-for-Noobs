# 🖥️ Server Types: Which EverQuest Should You Play?

One of the most confusing things about coming to EverQuest in 2024+ is that there are **multiple very different versions** of the game running simultaneously. This guide explains each option so you can make an informed choice.

---

## 🌍 The Three Main Options

| Server Type | Best For | Difficulty | Community |
|------------|---------|------------|-----------|
| 🟢 **EQLive** (retail) | New players wanting modern conveniences | Easier | Moderate |
| 🔵 **TLP** (Time-Locked Progression) | The "classic feel" with a fresh community | Hard | Very active at launch |
| 🔴 **Project 1999** (free, unofficial) | Purists who want the true 1999–2001 experience | Very Hard | Dedicated |

---

## 🟢 EQLive (Live Servers)

**Website:** daybreakgames.com / everquest.com
**Cost:** Free to play up to level 85; All Access subscription (~$15/month) for full content

### What It Is
EQLive is the **official, fully-updated version** of EverQuest, maintained by Daybreak Game Company. It has all 30+ expansions, modern UI improvements, quality-of-life features, and decades of content.

### The Good
- ✅ **Free to play up to level 85** — a massive amount of content before needing a subscription
- ✅ **Modern conveniences** — advanced loot window, mercenaries (NPC companions), better pathfinding
- ✅ **Mercenaries** — hire NPC healers, tanks, or DPS to fill group roles when you can't find players. Game-changing for solo play.
- ✅ **Huge amount of content** — 30+ expansions worth of zones, quests, and raids
- ✅ **Cross-server chat** — easier to find people
- ✅ **Officially supported** — patches, bug fixes, occasional new content

### The Bad
- ❌ **Population is older and spread thin** — players are scattered across 30 expansions worth of content
- ❌ **Finding groups for classic-era content is hard** — most players are at endgame
- ❌ **Mercs change the game significantly** — some feel it removes the need for social grouping
- ❌ **Power creep** — years of expansions have made early content trivially easy
- ❌ **Complex legacy systems** — 25+ years of additions make some systems confusing

### Who Should Play EQLive
- Players who want to **play at their own pace** without worrying about server resets
- Players who **want to solo** effectively (Mercs make this viable)
- Players interested in **current endgame raiding**
- Players who want **all the modern QoL features**

## 🔵 TLP Servers (Time-Locked Progression)

**Website:** daybreakgames.com (same as EQLive, separate server selection)
**Cost:** Requires All Access subscription (~$15/month)

### What It Is
TLP servers **start at the beginning** (original EverQuest, 1999) and **unlock new expansions over time** — typically every few months based on player voting or a fixed schedule. They give you the classic experience with a fresh, active community.

### How TLP Works
1. Server launches with **Classic EQ content only** (original zones, no Kunark, no Velious)
2. Players level up, raid classic content
3. After a set period, the community votes or automatically unlocks **Ruins of Kunark** (first expansion)
4. Then **Scars of Velious**, then **Shadows of Luclin**, and so on
5. Eventually the server "catches up" to live content (some TLPs then merge into live)

### TLP Rulesets Vary
Daybreak releases new TLP servers periodically with different rules:

| Ruleset | What It Means |
|---------|--------------|
| **True Box** | Can only play 1 account per computer (anti-2-boxing) |
| **Mischief / Free Trade** | Items are tradeable even if normally NO DROP |
| **Hardcore Heritage** | Extra challenging rules |
| **Yelinak / Vaniki** | Specific ruleset experiments |
| **Oakwynd / Teek** | Recent TLPs with unique rule mixes |

### The Good
- ✅ **Massive population at launch** — TLP launches are packed, groups are everywhere
- ✅ **Fresh economy** — no inflation from years of farming
- ✅ **Classic content is relevant again** — zones that are empty on live are packed on TLP
- ✅ **Community events** — server-first kills, drama, history being made
- ✅ **The "correct" way** to experience EQ's classic era in sequence

### The Bad
- ❌ **Subscription required** — no free play option
- ❌ **Population drops significantly** after the initial rush — many players quit after 1–3 expansions
- ❌ **Pay-to-win concerns** — some players use boxing software or buy plat/items
- ❌ **Server eventually ages** — starts fresh but ends up a ghost town 2–3 years in

### Who Should Play TLP
- Players who want the **full classic EQ experience** with a real population
- Players interested in **racing through content** as the server unlocks
- Returning veterans who want **nostalgia with company**
- Players willing to pay the subscription

## 🔴 Project 1999 (P1999)

**Website:** project1999.com
**Cost:** Completely free (fan-run, no official affiliation)

### What It Is
Project 1999 is a **free, fan-maintained private server** that permanently recreates EverQuest as it was in **1999–2001** (pre-Planes of Power era). It is NOT affiliated with Daybreak and operates under a negotiated agreement.

There are two servers:
- **P1999 Green** — newer server, started in 2019, currently in Velious/Luclin era
- **P1999 Blue** — the original server, in a late-era state with long-standing population

### The Good
- ✅ **Completely free** — no subscription, no cash shop
- ✅ **True classic experience** — plays exactly like 1999–2001 EQ, bugs and all
- ✅ **No mercs, no modern shortcuts** — pure original gameplay
- ✅ **Dedicated community** — smaller but extremely passionate
- ✅ **Stable** — been running for 15+ years with consistent rules
- ✅ **No 2-boxing allowed** (officially) — forces genuine social interaction

### The Bad
- ❌ **No modern QoL features** — no advanced loot window, no mercs, no cross-server tools
- ❌ **Very hardcore** — this is EQ at its most punishing
- ❌ **Small population** — harder to find groups, especially at odd hours
- ❌ **Content is capped** — stops at Velious/Luclin, no later expansions
- ❌ **Unofficial** — no GM support for technical issues; relies on volunteer staff
- ❌ **Raid content is very competitive** — small group of guilds dominate targets

### Who Should Play P1999
- Players who want **the authentic 1999 experience** exactly as it was
- Players who want **free access** without any subscription
- Players who value **pure social gameplay** with no shortcuts
- Hardcore EQ veterans or history enthusiasts

## 🤔 My Recommendation

**If you're brand new and unsure:** Start with **EQLive** — it's free, you can try it without commitment, and mercenaries make it viable to learn the game solo before grouping. The free content alone will last you months.

**If you want the authentic social experience:** Jump into a **new TLP launch**. The population density makes grouping easy, and the classic content is perfectly paced. Check EQ forums to see which TLP recently launched.

**If you want the real thing, free, and pure:** **P1999** is the most authentic EQ experience available. Be prepared for a harder time finding groups and a more demanding community. The wiki (wiki.project1999.com) is excellent.
