# 🤝 Grouping, Roles & Finding a Group in EverQuest

EverQuest was built around groups. Most content — especially the best XP camps and all dungeon content — is balanced for **6 players**. Learning to find, join, and contribute to a group is one of the most important skills in the game. This guide covers everything from finding a group to understanding your role once you're in one.

---

## 👥 Why Group?

EQ's grouping benefits are significant and immediate:

| Solo | Group of 6 |
|------|-----------|
| Kill 1 mob at a time | Kill 2–3 mobs at a time efficiently |
| Limited to mobs you can handle alone | Access harder content with better loot |
| Slow mana/HP recovery between pulls | Dedicated healer keeps you going |
| Die if you make a mistake | Tank absorbs mistakes |
| Limited to classes that solo well | Any class works |

Even a mediocre group with strangers is usually better XP than solo for most classes.

## 🎭 Group Composition: Roles

A standard EQ group has 6 players filling these roles:

### 🛡️ Tank (1 required)
**Classes:** Warrior, Shadow Knight, Paladin (in that order of tanking ability)
- Keeps enemies **focused on them** (taunting)
- Absorbs damage so others don't
- Sets the pace — the group assists the tank's target
- **Never steal the tank's target** — this is one of EQ's cardinal rules

### 💚 Healer (1 required)
**Classes:** Cleric (best), Druid (good), Shaman (good utility)
- Keeps the tank alive with heals
- Must **manage mana carefully** — a healer who runs dry kills the group
- Should be focused on the tank's health, not dealing damage
- Groups slow down or sit between pulls to let the healer recover mana

### ❄️ Crowd Control (CC) (1, very valuable)
**Classes:** Enchanter (best), Bard, Druid (root), Necromancer (root)
- **Mezzmerizes** (mezzes) or roots extra mobs so the group fights one at a time
- Critical in dungeons where pulls often bring 2–3 mobs
- **The rest of the group must NOT break the mez** — hitting a mezzed mob wakes it up
- Enchanters also provide mana regeneration buffs (Clarity/Breeze) — extremely valuable

### ⚔️ DPS (2–3 slots)
**Classes:** Wizard, Rogue, Monk, Ranger, Necromancer, Magician, Bard, Berserker
- Deals damage to kill mobs faster
- Should **assist the main tank** — type `/assist <tankname>` and attack the same mob
- Don't nuke randomly — help focus fire on the tank's target

### 🔧 Utility / Hybrid (flexible)
**Classes:** Shaman (slow + buffs), Bard (songs + CC), Beastlord, Ranger
- Many classes fill multiple roles
- Shamans are especially valued for **Slow** (reduces monster attack speed dramatically)
- Bards are perpetually welcome — their songs benefit the entire group passively

## 🚩 Setting the LFG Flag

Before spamming zone chat, **set your LFG (Looking For Group) flag**. This makes you visible to players using `/who all lfg`.

### How to Set LFG
- Press **Alt+G** to open the Group window
- Check the **"LFG"** checkbox
- Or type: `/lfg on`

To turn it off: `/lfg off`

### Checking Who's LFG
```
/who all lfg
```
Shows all players on the server with LFG enabled, their level, class, and zone.

```
/who all lfg 20 30
```
Shows LFG players between levels 20 and 30.

## 📢 How to Find a Group

### Method 1: Ask in Zone Chat
The most common method:
```
/ooc 23 Cleric LFG - can heal any camp in this zone
```
Format: **[Level] [Class] LFG - [what you can do or where you want to go]**

Be specific about what you offer. "23 Cleric LFG heals" gets groups faster than just "LFG."

### Method 2: Use /who to Find Active Groups
```
/who all 20 30
```
Find players near your level in zones you want to camp. Then `/tell` them:
> *"Hey, I'm a 23 Cleric LFG — are you guys looking for a healer?"*

### Method 3: Go to a Camp and Ask
Walk into a popular camp area and ask if the group needs your class. Worst they can say is no.

### Method 4: The Group Window (EQLive)
EQLive has a built-in **Looking for Group tool** in the Group window (Alt+G) that shows nearby LFG players with their class and level — essentially a simplified LFG browser.

## 📨 Inviting and Joining Groups

### To Invite Someone
```
/invite <playername>
```
Or right-click their name in chat → Invite to Group.

### To Accept an Invite
A group invite window will pop up — click **Accept**.

### To Leave a Group
```
/disband
```
Or right-click your own group frame → Disband. Be courteous — give advance warning if you need to leave.

### To Kick a Member (Group Leader Only)
Right-click their portrait in the group window → Remove from Group.

## 🧭 Group Etiquette: How Not to Get Blacklisted

EQ has a tight-knit community. Your reputation follows you. These rules prevent you from being the person no one wants to group with:

### ✅ Do These Things
- **Assist the main tank.** Type `/assist <MT>` and attack what they're attacking.
- **Announce when you need to go AFK.** Even 5 minutes without warning can cause a wipe.
- **Say when you're low on mana/health.** "OOM" (out of mana) and "LOM" (low on mana) are standard callouts.
- **Ask before looting disputed items.** "Can I roll on the Ebon Mace?" goes a long way.
- **Contribute to pulls.** Don't just stand there — engage with the group's rhythm.
- **Thank the healer and tank** when you leave. They take the most stress.

### ❌ Don't Do These Things
- **Don't break mez.** If an Enchanter or Bard has a mob locked down (mezzed), don't touch it. Ever.
- **Don't ninja loot.** Taking items without group agreement is a reputation killer.
- **Don't go AFK mid-pull.** The group can die because you disappeared.
- **Don't pull without telling the group.** "Pulling!" is a two-second callout that prevents chaos.
- **Don't spam your biggest spells right away.** You'll run out of resources and become a liability.
- **Don't rage-quit after a death.** Wipes happen. Staying to recover shows character.

## 🗣️ Essential Group Communication

Short callouts everyone should know:

| Callout | Meaning |
|---------|---------|
| **OOM** | Out of mana — healer/caster needs to stop and sit |
| **LOM** | Low on mana — warning, reduce incoming pulls |
| **INC** | Incoming — extra mob heading to camp |
| **Mezz'd** | Mob is crowd-controlled — DO NOT ATTACK IT |
| **Mez broke** | CC is down — mob is now active |
| **Pulling!** | About to go get a mob — get ready |
| **Sit** | Everyone sit for mana/HP regen |
| **MT** | Main Tank — the player everyone assists |
| **MA** | Main Assist — the player whose target DPS should attack (often same as MT) |
| **FD** | Feign Death (Monks/Necros) — they're faking death to drop aggro |
| **LD** | Link Dead — player lost connection |
| **BRB** | Be right back |
| **AFK** | Away from keyboard |
